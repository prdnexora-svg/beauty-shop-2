# Beauty Shop / Nexora Luxe — audit and cleanup

Date: 2026-09-06

Repository: `prdnexora-svg/beauty-shop-2`

Baseline: `1038fd61f4c8ef2cfd19ed0e1acbbc289700fcc2`

## Outcome

The existing purple/gold visual design has been retained. Confirmed navigation, catalogue, guest-auth and redundant-UI problems are corrected in this branch. The app is **not yet verified production-ready**: several business workflows still operate on bundled fixtures or browser storage rather than a complete shared backend.

This is a source audit plus a focused live public-flow audit, not certification that every screen works end to end. No production database changes, real messages, purchases, account creation or live authentication were performed.
